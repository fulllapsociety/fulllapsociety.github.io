Privacy Policy

Full Lap Society Poster — operated by Full Lap Society
Last updated: October 17, 2026

1. What this app is

Full Lap Society Poster is a private tool that Full Lap Society, a motorsports content brand, uses to publish its own racing videos and photos to its own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts or sign-ups.

2. Information we access

When the Full Lap Society TikTok account is connected, the app receives only what TikTok's authorization provides for the permissions granted: basic profile information (such as display name and avatar) and the ability to upload or post videos to that account. We do not access, collect, or store personal information about other TikTok users.

3. How we use it

We use this access only to post Full Lap Society's own content to its own account after the owner approves each post. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or analytics about individuals.

4. Storage and sharing

Access tokens are kept private by the account owner and are used only to operate the app. We do not sell, rent, or share information with third parties, except as needed to send content to TikTok through its official APIs or as required by law.

5. Retention and removal

Tokens are kept only while the connection is active. The owner can disconnect the app at any time in TikTok's app settings, which revokes its access, and can request deletion of any stored data by emailing the contact below.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page.

8. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: fulllapsociety@gmail.com
